Five things only you can make. Everything else is captured or built by script. Put every file in `~/leaf-video/mine/` with the names below, then tell me they're there.

## 1. The sick plant (video)

The plant must be your own and the damage real: spots, yellowing, holes, mildew. October is good for this, because older leaves usually have something. Don't pick off healthy leaves to make a point.

**Phone settings (iPhone)**
1. Settings → Camera → Record Video → **4K at 60 fps**.
2. Settings → Camera → Formats → **Most Compatible**. This makes photos JPEG, which Leaf accepts (it can't read HEIC).
3. On iPhone 13 Pro or later: Settings → Camera → turn **Macro Control** on, so the camera doesn't jump lenses mid-shot.
4. Turn off Live Photos and any filters. Clean the lens.

**Light.** Bright shade or near a window with indirect light. Avoid direct sun, which burns out the spots, and avoid flash. Overcast outdoor light is ideal.

**How to shoot**
1. Tap and hold on the damaged leaf until **AE/AF LOCK** appears. That stops focus and brightness from pumping.
2. Hold the phone with both hands, elbows tucked in, at 1x (or 2x if your phone has it). Don't use digital zoom.
3. Record each shot for **8 to 10 seconds**, and do **3 takes** of each:
   - **Close-up 1:** fill the frame with the worst leaf. Hold still for 3 s, then move in slowly for 5 s.
   - **Close-up 2:** the same leaf from a slightly different angle, still, 8 s.
   - **Wide:** the whole plant, with the damaged area visible, still, 8 s.
   - **Underside:** turn one damaged leaf over gently, still, 8 s.
4. No talking while filming. The audio from these clips is not used.

## 2. The photos Leaf will diagnose

These are the photos uploaded in the real run on the live site, so take them the way a gardener would.

1. With the same settings, take a **photo** (not video) of: the close-up of the damage, the whole plant, and the underside of a leaf.
2. AirDrop them to your Mac. Check they end in `.jpg`. If they're `.heic`, Formats wasn't set to Most Compatible; retake them.
3. Write one sentence of what you've noticed, the way you'd tell a friend (for example: "brown spots with yellow rings on the lower leaves, spreading up after a week of rain"). Put it in `~/leaf-video/mine/notes.txt`. This is the note typed in the run.

## 3. A push notification arriving on your phone

This uses Leaf's real **Send a test** button. The video labels it "Real push · test button", so it's honest about what it is.

**Set up once**
1. On the iPhone, open **leafathon.site/today** in Safari.
2. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen → Add**.
3. Open Leaf **from the Home Screen icon**, not from Safari. Push only works there, and there's no address bar to leak your garden link.
4. Create a garden (any name, your town) and add one plant.
5. Under **Notifications**, tap **Turn on notifications** and allow them.
6. Settings → Notifications → Leaf: turn on **Banners**, and set **Show Previews** to **Always**.
7. Turn off every **Focus** mode (including Do Not Disturb and Sleep).
8. Settings → Control Center: add **Screen Recording** if it isn't there.

**Record**
1. Open Control Center and tap **Screen Recording**. Wait for the 3-second countdown.
2. Open Leaf from the Home Screen, scroll to Notifications, and tap **Send a test**.
3. Swipe up to the Home Screen straight away and wait. The banner "Leaf notifications are on" should arrive within a few seconds.
4. Let it sit for 3 s, then stop recording from the red pill at the top.
5. Do 3 takes. AirDrop the best one as `push.mov`.

If nothing arrives after two tries, tell me. The fallbacks are an Android phone with Chrome, or Chrome on the Mac.

## 4. The narration

Record on the Mac's built-in microphone. **Not AirPods**: macOS switches them to a low-quality call codec.

**Room.** The smallest, softest room you have. A closet with clothes or a bedroom with curtains is much better than a kitchen. Turn off fans and air conditioning, and close windows.

**Setup**
1. Open QuickTime Player → **File → New Audio Recording**.
2. Click the small arrow next to the record button: choose **MacBook Pro Microphone** (or your Mac's name) and **Quality: Maximum**.
3. Start recording, then open **Control Center → Mic Mode** and choose **Voice Isolation**. Mic Mode only appears while the mic is in use.
4. Sit with the laptop 30 to 40 cm away, screen at eye level. Use a glass of water, not coffee.

**Record**
1. Read from the teleprompter block in [`VIDEO_PLAN.md`](VIDEO_PLAN.md), section 4. Read it aloud twice before recording.
2. **Stay silent for 2 seconds** at the start of every file. That silence is used to measure the room noise.
3. Record each section as its own file, 3 takes each, in a calm, normal speaking voice. Don't perform, and don't rush; a little slower than conversation is right.
4. If you stumble, pause, then say the whole sentence again. The editor keeps the clean one.
5. Save as `vo-1-hook.m4a`, `vo-2-what.m4a`, `vo-3-run.m4a`, `vo-4-garden.m4a`, `vo-5-built.m4a`, `vo-6-close.m4a`. Add `-take2`, `-take3` for the other takes.

**The verdict line.** Record the bracketed verdict line in all three versions (Likely, Send a sample, Confident) and save them as `vo-3-verdict-likely.m4a`, `vo-3-verdict-lab.m4a` and `vo-3-verdict-confident.m4a`. We don't know the verdict until the real run happens, and I won't fake one.

## 5. Music (optional)

My recommendation is **no music**: a quiet room tone fits the calm, first-person tone, and there's nothing to clear. If you want a bed:

1. Open YouTube Studio → **Audio Library**.
2. Filter by **Attribution: not required**, **Mood: Calm**, **Genre: Ambient**, and **Duration** over 3:00.
3. Pick one without vocals or a strong beat. Download it.
4. Take a **screenshot** of the track's row showing "attribution not required", and save it with the track.

It will sit under the voice at about −20 dB, low enough that it's felt more than heard.

## Before you send anything

- No garden links, keys or email addresses visible on any screen recording.
- Phone screen recordings come from the Home Screen app, not a Safari tab.
- Footage is of your own plant only.
