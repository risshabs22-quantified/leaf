        How Leaf thought

        Every model call runs on Nebius Token Factory. NVIDIA Nemotron does the reasoning and writing.

        StepModelTimeTokens in / out

        Four steps, each by the model best suited to it.

        Leaf shows its work. Every step is listed with the model that ran it and how long it took.

        1. Look

          A vision model reads the photo and lists only what it can see: the plant, the symptoms, and where they appear. It does not diagnose.

        2. Reason

          Nemotron Ultra weighs the likely causes against the evidence for and against each, and asks follow-up questions when causes look alike.

        3. Search

          Tavily searches for extension-office and university guidance, so advice rests on sources you can open and read.

        4. Write

          Nemotron Lightning turns the diagnosis and sources into a short care plan, with a date to check back in.

        Leaf gives guidance, not certainty.

        When Leaf isn't confident, it says so and points you to your local extension office. For food crops and any chemical, follow the product label.
