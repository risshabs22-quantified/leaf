Leaf is a plant doctor. Show it a sick leaf and it weighs the likely causes, searches extension-office guides, and writes you a care plan that cites its sources.

It can tell early blight from a nutrient problem, work out why pepper leaves turn yellow, or look into a white powder on squash. When it isn't sure, it says so.
