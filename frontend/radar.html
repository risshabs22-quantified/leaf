    How Radar works

    Radar adds at most 5 points to a diagnosis candidate that matches a report in your state, and never enough to make Leaf confident on its own. It runs on weekday mornings for states that have gardens (each once a week), and reads at most 10 pages a state a week to stay inside Leaf's free search credits.
