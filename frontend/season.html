Write my season plan

Leaf plans every week of the next twelve months for your plants: sowing, planting out, feeding, pruning, protection and harvest. It is built on 30 years of your area's frost dates and each plant's sourced facts. Nemotron Ultra writes it a month at a time; code rules and Nemotron Super check every item before you see it. It takes a minute or two.
